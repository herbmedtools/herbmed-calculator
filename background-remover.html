How to Remove Image Backgrounds cleanly

Use the HerbMed Tools Background Remover to separate subjects from cluttered or solid backgrounds for product listings, profile avatars, and blog graphics.

  • Upload your photo: Choose a clear image with good contrast between the subject and background.
  • Automatic edge detection: The tool processes the image and previews transparency on a checkerboard grid.
  • Touch up fine spots: Switch between Eraser to remove leftover background patches and Restore Original to bring back any clipped detail.
  • Export PNG: Download the final image with a transparent background ready for design or publishing.
