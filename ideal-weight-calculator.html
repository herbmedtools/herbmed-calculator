How Does the Ideal Weight by Age Calculator Work?

Healthy body weight depends on age, gender, and height. For children and teenagers (ages 1 to 18), growth charts based on age and biological sex provide an accurate healthy weight range. For adults aged 19 and older, height becomes the primary factor alongside age and body frame.

How to Use This Tool

  • Select Gender: Choose Male or Female as body composition differs slightly between genders.
  • Enter Age: Type your age in years (from 1 to 100).
  • Enter Height: For adults (19+), enter your height in feet and inches for a precise calculation.
  • Current Weight (Optional): Add your current weight in kilograms (kg) to see whether you are underweight, normal, or overweight.

Why Maintaining a Healthy Weight Matters

Keeping your weight within a healthy range supports heart health, balanced hormones, glowing skin, and better energy levels. Pair balanced nutrition with regular physical activity for sustainable wellness.
