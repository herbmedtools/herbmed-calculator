How to Use Image Studio

Create clean social media graphics, blog visuals, and photo collages directly in your browser without uploading files to an external server.

  • Text Overlay: Enter your caption, choose a font style, pick a color or background box, and adjust size and position.
  • Watermark: Add brand text or a logo image, choose opacity, and place it in any corner or center.
  • Collage & Export: Upload two photos for side-by-side or stacked layouts, add spacing or rounded corners, and download as PNG or JPG.
